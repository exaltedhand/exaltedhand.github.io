Wash your clothes with cold water. You can make an exception for bedsheets.

Put the dishwasher pod inside the detergent tray. Its literally printed on the box.

Use a package manager, even on windows. Try using Winget. Tbh there aren't many great options.

Use a password manager as well. Lots of decent options, I use BitWarden.

Automatically backup everything. Automatic backups have saved my ass more than once. I use Macrium Reflect for this one, although bear in mind its EoS.

Keeping your shit under 65kb lets you cram everything into a single packet.

Massgrave lets you upgrade your Windows installation without paying the Microsoft extortion racket. Also useful for access to the suite formerly known as Microsoft Office.

The Big-Ass Data Broker Opt-Out List (BADBOOL) is great for privacy freaks. Just deal with the stuff marked crucial

You can use DNS-level adblocking to block ads and telemetry on mobile. I use NextDNS.
